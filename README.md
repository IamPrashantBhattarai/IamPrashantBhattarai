## 🔬 Research Interests

I want to use machine learning to make scientific models **faster and more trustworthy**: learned surrogates that stand in for expensive simulations, and real-time forecasts that report their own uncertainty. Growing up in landslide-prone Nepal, I am most drawn to **natural-hazard early warning**. I hope to ground this work in numerical methods and high-performance computing through a **PhD in Computational Science**, while continuing to teach the core ideas of computing, as I do today.

`Scientific ML` · `Surrogate Modelling` · `Uncertainty Quantification` · `Explainable AI` · `Remote Sensing` · `Geohazards`

## 🧪 Research Projects

### 🏔️ Explainability of Landslide Detection — *Oct 2026 – present*
<sub>PyTorch · Sentinel-2 · Landslide4Sense</sub>

- I compare a 14-band **ResNet-18** with a **DINOv3** satellite vision transformer for landslide detection on multispectral Sentinel-2 imagery.
- I test **Grad-CAM, Integrated Gradients, RISE, occlusion and attention rollout** against ground-truth landslide masks. The tests cover localization, deletion/insertion faithfulness, model-randomization sanity checks, band-level spectral analysis and shortcut tests.
- **Question:** do the models rely on physically meaningful evidence, or on shortcuts?

### 🏥 Clinical NLP: Hospital Readmission Prediction — *Mar – Apr 2026*
<sub>Python · NLP · SHAP · FastAPI</sub>

- An end-to-end pipeline that predicts 30-day readmission from discharge summaries. It includes topic modelling, a fairness audit and SHAP explanations, and is served through a FastAPI dashboard.

### 📚 IntelliDocs: Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation — *Undergraduate thesis, 2025*
<sub>Python · OCR · BERT · BLIP · CLIP</sub>

- Three coordinated pipelines: semantic text retrieval (OCR → chunking → embeddings → vector search), a fine-tuned BERT query router, and multimodal image retrieval that fuses Tesseract, BLIP and CLIP ViT-B/16.
- **Recall@5 = 92% · MAP = 0.89 · 2.7 s average latency.** This work is the basis of the JoEIS paper.

### ⚛️ Quantum Tic-Tac-Toe — *2024*
<sub>Qiskit · Python</sub>

- Simulates superposition and entanglement with IBM Qiskit, combined with alpha–beta pruning for adversarial search.
